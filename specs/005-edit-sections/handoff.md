## Delivered

Each catalog card has a sibling labelled edit icon, revealed on hover or keyboard
focus and always shown for no-hover devices. The `edit-section/:sectionId` route
reuses the authoring page with fresh GET loading and the published PATCH request.
Title, numeric summary and both image paths are editable; failed reads/updates
are explicit and recoverable. Successful updates replace the keyed catalog record
and the selected section state, then return to the mounted catalog. Pending and
already-confirmed saves cannot be repeated.
